**Use:** Adapt this message for an official vendor contact form or authorized design partner. It is an exploratory inquiry, not a claim that a commercial company exists.

**Subject:** Feasibility inquiry — custom Android smartphone platform and reference design

Hello,

I am an independent student in Brazil researching the feasibility of a compact custom Android smartphone concept called TITAN Compact Pro. This is currently an exploratory project; no company has been incorporated and no prototype has been built.

The initial design goals include an approximately 6.3-inch OLED display, an active stylus integrated into the bottom edge, three rear cameras (main, ultrawide, and telephoto), and an Android-based software stack using AOSP where feasible.

I would like to understand whether your team supports independent/student-led feasibility projects or whether this type of work must be handled through a registered company or authorized design partner.

Could you please advise on:
1. The appropriate team or authorized partner for custom mobile-device development.
2. Availability and eligibility requirements for a supported mobile SoC platform and reference design.
3. Access to Android BSP, kernel/drivers, camera ISP/HAL integration, modem/RF documentation, and security-update support.
4. NDA/licensing requirements, minimum order quantities, development costs, and realistic next steps.
5. Whether there is a recommended path for a low-volume feasibility prototype.

I understand that platform access, proprietary documentation, and engineering support may be restricted and may require commercial agreements. I am not assuming that any particular chipset or component is available until confirmed by your team.

Thank you for directing me to the appropriate contact.

Regards,
