# Roadmap

The project should advance through evidence-based gates, not by purchasing parts first.

## Phase 0 — Define the concept
- [x] Record initial product targets.
- [x] Identify major unknowns.
- [ ] Sketch front, back, sides, stylus slot, and camera island.
- [ ] Mark each requirement as essential, negotiable, or optional.

**Exit condition:** Requirements are consistent and measurable.

## Phase 1 — Platform feasibility
- [ ] Contact platform vendors and authorized design partners.
- [ ] Verify SoC naming, availability, eligibility, licensing, and cost.
- [ ] Confirm BSP, kernel, camera HAL/ISP, modem/RF, security, and reference-design access.
- [ ] Record responses and sources without publishing confidential details.

**Exit condition:** At least one plausible supported platform path is confirmed in writing.

## Phase 2 — Component feasibility
- [ ] Research compatible OLED + digitizer.
- [ ] Research active stylus technology and integration.
- [ ] Shortlist camera modules with complete technical evidence.
- [ ] Research battery, PMIC, memory, storage, sensors, audio, haptics, and connectivity.
- [ ] Build a first-pass mass, volume, power, thermal, and cost budget.

**Exit condition:** No critical subsystem depends only on an unverified assumption.

## Phase 3 — Mechanical and electrical architecture
- [ ] Create CAD envelope and component stack-up.
- [ ] Check camera depth, stylus storage, battery volume, antenna zones, and thermal paths.
- [ ] Develop block diagram and interface matrix.
- [ ] Review with qualified hardware engineers or a design partner.

**Exit condition:** A credible reference design and integration plan exist.

## Phase 4 — Prototype planning
- [ ] Obtain written quotations and development terms.
- [ ] Define test plan and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Establish battery, RF, electrical, thermal, and ESD safety reviews.
- [ ] Build only with suitable engineering support and approved parts.

**Exit condition:** Budget, sourcing, safety, and legal requirements are understood.

## Phase 5 — Prototype and software
- [ ] Bring up the board on supported vendor software.
- [ ] Validate display, stylus, cameras, connectivity, power, thermal, audio, and sensors.
- [ ] Build the custom Android experience incrementally.
- [ ] Establish OTA and security-update maintenance.

## Phase 6 — Product readiness
- [ ] Validate reliability and manufacturing test procedures.
- [ ] Plan applicable regulatory approvals and carrier requirements.
- [ ] Confirm repairability, supply continuity, and support commitments.

A finished consumer phone is a long-term possibility, not a guaranteed outcome. If the platform-access gate fails, reconsider the scope before spending money.
